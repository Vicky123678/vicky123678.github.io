Jiangxi Provincial People's Congress Standing Committee Deputy Director Ji 'an Municipal Party Committee Secretary Hu Shizhong and his party came to visit Xusheng Electronics

    Morning of April 17, 2019, jiangxi province deputy director of the National People's Congress standing committee of the municipal party committee secretary Hu Shizhong along with provincial financial committee, the provincial Ministry of Industry and Information Technology hall, the province science-education-culture-health market supervision and administration committee, province, provincial federation of industry and commerce, the provincial federation of enterprises, provincial revenue related to the leading comrades visited Xusheng electronic Co., LTD., vice secretary of the county, the county magistrate Chen Kelong accompanied by inspection.

Mr. Lu Chongyang, the general manager of Xusheng Electronics, on behalf of all the staff of Xusheng , extended a warm welcome to Mr. Hu and his party, and introduced the general situation of Xusheng Electronics, product research and development, technology and equipment strength, market layout, future planning, etc. Mr. Lu said that Jiangxi is in an important period of high-quality development, with superior business environment and broad space for cooperation. On the other hand, PCB industry has great development potential and has become a global industry. Xusheng Electronics will seize the opportunity and make new contributions to the economic development of Jiangxi.

Into Xusheng production workshop, Mr. Lu introduces Xusheng have "scale advantage, automated production lines, high performance-to-price ratio" these three core competitiveness, Xusheng electronic PCB production in strict accordance with the "fast, high quality, protect" (i.e., delivery fast, cost-effective, industry leading quality and after-sale guarantee) standards, fully meet customer needs, dedicated to provide customers with more than expected product value, realizes the industry leading price.

    After listening to Mr. Lu's introduction of Xusheng Electronics, Mr. Hu expressed his high appreciation for the core competitiveness of Xusheng Electronics, and his sincere admiration for Xusheng Electronics' progress in the PCB field and its dedication to research and development. He hoped that Xusheng Electronics, as a leading enterprise in PCB industry, would give full play to its advantages in technology and equipment, apply more cost-effective PCB boards to relevant industries in Jiangxi, continue reform and innovation, create better economic and social benefits, and lead the sound and rapid development of the province's economy.

    During this visit, the party and government leaders of Jiangxi Province fully demonstrated the achievements of the company in recent years, and laid a solid foundation for building a smooth bridge of political and commercial exchange and interaction. In the future, Xusheng will not entrust, improve the level of equipment production, closely combined with the government needs, and actively make due contributions to the local economic development. Based on Jiangxi, look to the whole country and even the world, to strengthen and improve Xusheng PCB products, is committed to become the authority of PCB industry and industry standard setting supplier.
